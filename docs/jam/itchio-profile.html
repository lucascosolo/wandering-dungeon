Now Playing: The Wandering Dungeon

The Wandering Dungeon is my entry for the Do You WANNA Jam?! 2026 (theme: unstable). It's a turn-based ASCII roguelite where the dungeon literally shifts around you — rooms slide, corridors reconnect, walls collapse.

Five unstable regions. Five bosses. One unkillable hunter that appears if you linger. All wrapped in a progressive web app — no install, plays in your browser, saves to your device.
